# Incentive Engine: 10-minute demo for NMPA's team

Open the dashboard, tab **Incentive Engine**. Speak to the numbers on screen; every figure comes from NMPA's own documents.

1. **Start here (2 min).** The four cards: wharfage earned this year, key customers at risk, cargo going to other ports,
   NMPA schemes in force. Then "This week's actions": each card says who should act, why (the numbers) and where to look.
   Show the one-page brief (PDF).
   *Say:* "This is what your Traffic team would open every Monday."
2. **What we earn (1 min).** The chart of wharfage by cargo, this year against last year. Click "How it works" to show that
   every figure is tonnes from your statements times your Scale of Rates, checked against your published accounts.
3. **Customers & cargo (3 min).** Key customers coloured by risk: MRPL is 58% of traced wharfage and its ships waited
   263 days because MRPL was not ready, so the first step is coordination, not a discount. Cargo lost to other ports:
   POL products' share fell from 10.2% to 7.8%. Seasonal windows: LPG's quiet months, when its berths are free.
   Price comparison: NMPA's wharfage is below Cochin's and VOC's on almost every line.
   *Say:* "Price is rarely the reason here; the tool shows what to check first."
4. **Proposals & schemes (3 min).** Your two schemes in force, and the iron ore rebate so far. The discount calculator:
   move the slider; the red line (discount on all cargo) only breaks even at 11% more cargo, the green line (rebate on
   extra cargo only) gains from the first extra tonne. Customer proposal: one page per customer, saved as a draft that goes
   Traffic review, Finance review, approval.
   *Say:* "Nothing goes to a customer without your approval, and every step is recorded."
5. **Close (1 min).** What the tool needs from NMPA to turn estimates into checked figures: billed wharfage by cargo,
   customer name confirmation, container royalty terms, and a nodal officer.

If asked "is this accurate?": every rate is located in your Scale of Rates with its page number; 277 of 323 daily reports
are checked against two independent readings; money figures are labelled as estimates until NMPA billing is shared.
